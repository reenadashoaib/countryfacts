About this site

Country Facts is a quick reference for 250 countries and territories. Each page lists the capital, population, area, languages, currency, calling code and neighbours, with a short summary of how the country compares with others.

Where the data comes from

  • Population and GDP: the World Bank's World Development Indicators (licensed CC BY 4.0), via the datasets/population and datasets/gdp packages. Figures are for the latest year the World Bank has published. Some territories, such as Taiwan and Vatican City, have no World Bank figures, so we show them as not published.
  • Names, capitals, languages, currencies, borders and area: the mledoze/countries dataset, made available under the Open Database License. Country outlines are drawn from the same project's boundary files.
  • Flags: flag-icons by Panayiotis Lipiridis (MIT License).

Rankings and comparisons are calculated from these sources. Borders and names follow the dataset and are not a statement on any territorial dispute. Pages were last rebuilt on 2026-11-06.

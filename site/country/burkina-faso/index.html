Burkina Faso in numbers

Burkina Faso is the 76th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 59th largest by population.

It has roughly 88 people per square kilometre, about 1.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 101.9%, from 11.9 million to 24.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is New Zealand, at 270,467 km².

Burkina Faso is landlocked and shares land borders with 6 countries: Benin, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Mali, Niger and Togo.

The West African CFA franc is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $20.3 billion in 2023, or $883 per person. That ranks 196th of 213 by GDP per person.

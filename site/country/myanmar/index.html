Myanmar in numbers

Myanmar is the 41st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 27th largest by population.

It has roughly 81 people per square kilometre, about 1.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 21.1%, from 45.3 million to 54.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Afghanistan, at 652,230 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Bangladesh, China, India, Laos and Thailand.

Its economic output was about $64.8 billion in 2023, or $1,197 per person. That ranks 187th of 213 by GDP per person.

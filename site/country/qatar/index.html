Qatar in numbers

Qatar is the 165th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 138th largest by population.

It has roughly 257 people per square kilometre, about 4.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 360.8%, from 645 thousand to 3 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Falkland Islands, at 12,173 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Saudi Arabia.

Its economic output was about $235.8 billion in 2022, or $88,724 per person. That ranks 9th of 213 by GDP per person.

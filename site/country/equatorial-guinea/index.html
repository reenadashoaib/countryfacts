Equatorial Guinea in numbers

Equatorial Guinea is the 146th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 149th largest by population.

It has roughly 69 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 175.6%, from 703 thousand to 1.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Burundi, at 27,834 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Cameroon and Gabon.

The Central African CFA franc is also used in 5 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $12.1 billion in 2023, or $6,558 per person. That ranks 121st of 213 by GDP per person.

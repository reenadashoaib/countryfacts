Palestine in numbers

Palestine is the 173rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 123rd largest by population.

It has roughly 870 people per square kilometre, about 14.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 85.3%, from 2.9 million to 5.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Brunei, at 5,765 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Israel, Egypt and Jordan.

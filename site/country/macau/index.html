Macau in numbers

Macau is the 241st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 167th largest by population.

It has roughly 22,863 people per square kilometre, about 378.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 59.3%, from 431 thousand to 686 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Sint Maarten, at 34 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: China.

Its economic output was about $47.1 billion in 2023, or $69,331 per person. That ranks 14th of 213 by GDP per person.

Timor-Leste in numbers

Timor-Leste is the 160th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 154th largest by population.

It has roughly 95 people per square kilometre, about 1.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 90.4%, from 745 thousand to 1.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Bahamas, at 13,943 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Indonesia.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $2.2 billion in 2023, or $1,620 per person. That ranks 179th of 213 by GDP per person.

Pakistan in numbers

Pakistan is the 35th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 5th largest by population.

It has roughly 289 people per square kilometre, about 4.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 64.8%, from 154.9 million to 255.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Venezuela, at 916,445 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Afghanistan, China, India and Iran.

Its economic output was about $338.4 billion in 2023, or $1,367 per person. That ranks 184th of 213 by GDP per person.

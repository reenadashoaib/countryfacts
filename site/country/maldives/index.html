Maldives in numbers

Maldives is the 215th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 171st largest by population.

It has roughly 1,766 people per square kilometre, about 29.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 87.8%, from 282 thousand to 530 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Malta, at 316 km².

Maldives has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $6.6 billion in 2023, or $12,548 per person. That ranks 91st of 213 by GDP per person.

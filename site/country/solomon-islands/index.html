Solomon Islands in numbers

Solomon Islands is the 144th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 163rd largest by population.

It has roughly 29 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 90.4%, from 440 thousand to 839 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Albania, at 28,748 km².

Solomon Islands has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $1.6 billion in 2023, or $2,039 per person. That ranks 170th of 213 by GDP per person.

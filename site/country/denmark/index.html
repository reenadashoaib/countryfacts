Denmark in numbers

Denmark is the 134th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 114th largest by population.

It has roughly 139 people per square kilometre, about 2.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 12.5%, from 5.3 million to 6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Netherlands, at 41,850 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Germany.

The Danish krone is also used in 2 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $404.2 billion in 2023, or $67,967 per person. That ranks 15th of 213 by GDP per person.

Comoros in numbers

Comoros is the 183rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 162nd largest by population.

It has roughly 474 people per square kilometre, about 7.9 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 64.7%, from 536 thousand to 883 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Mauritius, at 2,040 km².

Comoros has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $1.4 billion in 2023, or $1,590 per person. That ranks 181st of 213 by GDP per person.

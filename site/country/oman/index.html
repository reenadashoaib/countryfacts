Oman in numbers

Oman is the 73rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 120th largest by population.

It has roughly 18 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 140.8%, from 2.3 million to 5.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Poland, at 312,679 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates and Yemen.

Its economic output was about $108.2 billion in 2023, or $21,427 per person. That ranks 67th of 213 by GDP per person.

Chad in numbers

Chad is the 22nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 63rd largest by population.

It has roughly 16 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 146.8%, from 8.5 million to 21 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Peru, at 1,285,216 km².

Chad is landlocked and shares land borders with 6 countries: Cameroon, Central African Republic, Libya, Niger, Nigeria and Sudan.

The Central African CFA franc is also used in 5 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $13.1 billion in 2023, or $681 per person. That ranks 201st of 213 by GDP per person.

Ghana in numbers

Ghana is the 83rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 47th largest by population.

It has roughly 147 people per square kilometre, about 2.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 78.6%, from 19.6 million to 35.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Romania, at 238,391 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Burkina Faso, Ivory Coast and Togo.

Its economic output was about $76.4 billion in 2023, or $2,260 per person. That ranks 165th of 213 by GDP per person.

Belgium in numbers

Belgium is the 141st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 81st largest by population.

It has roughly 391 people per square kilometre, about 6.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 16.5%, from 10.3 million to 11.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Lesotho, at 30,355 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: France, Germany, Luxembourg and Netherlands.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $632.2 billion in 2023, or $53,669 per person. That ranks 24th of 213 by GDP per person.

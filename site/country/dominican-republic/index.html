Dominican Republic in numbers

Dominican Republic is the 132nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 84th largest by population.

It has roughly 237 people per square kilometre, about 3.9 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 34.2%, from 8.6 million to 11.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Slovakia, at 49,037 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Haiti.

Its economic output was about $121.4 billion in 2023, or $10,718 per person. That ranks 97th of 213 by GDP per person.

Malaysia in numbers

Malaysia is the 69th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 45th largest by population.

It has roughly 109 people per square kilometre, about 1.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 56.6%, from 23 million to 36 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Vietnam, at 331,212 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Brunei, Indonesia and Thailand.

Its economic output was about $399.6 billion in 2023, or $11,377 per person. That ranks 94th of 213 by GDP per person.

Indonesia in numbers

Indonesia is the 16th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 4th largest by population.

It has roughly 150 people per square kilometre, about 2.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 32.2%, from 216.1 million to 285.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Sudan, at 1,886,068 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Timor-Leste, Malaysia and Papua New Guinea.

Its economic output was about $1.37 trillion in 2023, or $4,876 per person. That ranks 134th of 213 by GDP per person.

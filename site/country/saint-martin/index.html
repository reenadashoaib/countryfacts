Saint Martin in numbers

Saint Martin is the 235th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 213th largest by population.

It has roughly 471 people per square kilometre, about 7.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 16.9%, from 30 thousand to 25 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Bermuda, at 54 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Sint Maarten.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $649.2 million in 2021, or $21,668 per person. That ranks 66th of 213 by GDP per person.

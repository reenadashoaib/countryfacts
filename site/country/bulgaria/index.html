Bulgaria in numbers

Bulgaria is the 106th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 111th largest by population.

It has roughly 58 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 21.3%, from 8.2 million to 6.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Liberia, at 111,369 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Greece, North Macedonia, Romania, Serbia and Türkiye.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $101.6 billion in 2023, or $15,758 per person. That ranks 81st of 213 by GDP per person.

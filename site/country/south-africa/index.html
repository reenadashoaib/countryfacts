South Africa in numbers

South Africa is the 26th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 24th largest by population.

It has roughly 53 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 37.3%, from 47.2 million to 64.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Mali, at 1,240,192 km².

It shares land borders with 6 countries: Botswana, Lesotho, Mozambique, Namibia, Eswatini and Zimbabwe.

The South African rand is also used in 4 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $377.8 billion in 2023, or $5,976 per person. That ranks 126th of 213 by GDP per person.

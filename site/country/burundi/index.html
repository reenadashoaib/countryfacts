Burundi in numbers

Burundi is the 147th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 77th largest by population.

It has roughly 517 people per square kilometre, about 8.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 122.4%, from 6.5 million to 14.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Haiti, at 27,750 km².

Burundi is landlocked and shares land borders with 3 countries: DR Congo, Rwanda and Tanzania.

Its economic output was about $2.6 billion in 2023, or $193 per person. That ranks 213th of 213 by GDP per person.

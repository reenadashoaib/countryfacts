Mali in numbers

Mali is the 25th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 58th largest by population.

It has roughly 20 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 118%, from 11.6 million to 25.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Angola, at 1,246,700 km².

Mali is landlocked and shares land borders with 7 countries: Algeria, Burkina Faso, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Mauritania, Niger and Senegal.

The West African CFA franc is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $20.9 billion in 2023, or $879 per person. That ranks 197th of 213 by GDP per person.

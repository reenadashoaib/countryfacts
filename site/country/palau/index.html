Palau in numbers

Palau is the 202nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 214th largest by population.

It has roughly 38 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 7.9%, from 19 thousand to 18 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Northern Mariana Islands, at 464 km².

Palau has no land borders with other countries.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $263 million in 2023, or $14,837 per person. That ranks 82nd of 213 by GDP per person.

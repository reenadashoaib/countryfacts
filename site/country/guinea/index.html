Guinea in numbers

Guinea is the 80th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 74th largest by population.

It has roughly 61 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 79.1%, from 8.4 million to 15.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is United Kingdom, at 242,900 km².

It shares land borders with 6 countries: Ivory Coast, Guinea-Bissau, Liberia, Mali, Senegal and Sierra Leone.

Its economic output was about $23.6 billion in 2023, or $1,639 per person. That ranks 177th of 213 by GDP per person.

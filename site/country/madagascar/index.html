Madagascar in numbers

Madagascar is the 47th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 49th largest by population.

It has roughly 56 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 98.2%, from 16.5 million to 32.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Botswana, at 582,000 km².

Madagascar has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $16 billion in 2023, or $514 per person. That ranks 208th of 213 by GDP per person.

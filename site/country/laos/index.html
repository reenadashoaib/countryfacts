Laos in numbers

Laos is the 85th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 102nd largest by population.

It has roughly 33 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 45%, from 5.4 million to 7.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Romania, at 238,391 km².

Laos is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: Myanmar, Cambodia, China, Thailand and Vietnam.

Its economic output was about $15.8 billion in 2023, or $2,067 per person. That ranks 169th of 213 by GDP per person.

Rwanda in numbers

Rwanda is the 149th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 76th largest by population.

It has roughly 553 people per square kilometre, about 9.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 77.4%, from 8.2 million to 14.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is North Macedonia, at 25,713 km².

Rwanda is landlocked and shares land borders with 4 countries: Burundi, DR Congo, Tanzania and Uganda.

Its economic output was about $14.1 billion in 2023, or $1,010 per person. That ranks 193rd of 213 by GDP per person.

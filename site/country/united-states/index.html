United States in numbers

United States is the 5th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 3rd largest by population.

It has roughly 36 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 21.1%, from 282.2 million to 341.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is China, at 9,706,961 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Canada and Mexico.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $27.36 trillion in 2023, or $81,249 per person. That ranks 12th of 213 by GDP per person.

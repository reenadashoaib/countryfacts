Papua New Guinea in numbers

Papua New Guinea is the 56th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 91st largest by population.

It has roughly 23 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 94.4%, from 5.5 million to 10.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Cameroon, at 475,442 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Indonesia.

Its economic output was about $30.9 billion in 2023, or $2,977 per person. That ranks 156th of 213 by GDP per person.

Yemen in numbers

Yemen is the 51st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 37th largest by population.

It has roughly 79 people per square kilometre, about 1.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 112.9%, from 19.6 million to 41.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Thailand, at 513,120 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Oman and Saudi Arabia.

Its economic output was about $21.6 billion in 2018, or $634 per person. That ranks 205th of 213 by GDP per person.

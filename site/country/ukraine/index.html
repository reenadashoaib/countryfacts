Ukraine in numbers

Ukraine is the 46th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 40th largest by population.

It has roughly 65 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 21.3%, from 49.6 million to 39 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is South Sudan, at 619,745 km².

It shares land borders with 7 countries: Belarus, Hungary, Moldova, Poland, Romania, Russia and Slovakia.

Its economic output was about $178.8 billion in 2023, or $4,737 per person. That ranks 136th of 213 by GDP per person.

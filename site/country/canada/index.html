Canada in numbers

Canada is the 3rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 38th largest by population.

It has roughly 4.2 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 35.7%, from 30.7 million to 41.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is China, at 9,706,961 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: United States.

Its economic output was about $2.14 trillion in 2023, or $53,437 per person. That ranks 26th of 213 by GDP per person.

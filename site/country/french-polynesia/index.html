French Polynesia in numbers

French Polynesia is the 176th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 180th largest by population.

It has roughly 68 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 17.5%, from 240 thousand to 282 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Cape Verde, at 4,033 km².

French Polynesia has no land borders with other countries.

The CFP franc is also used in 2 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $5.8 billion in 2022, or $20,739 per person. That ranks 69th of 213 by GDP per person.

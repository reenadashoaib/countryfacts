Russia in numbers

Russia is the 1st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 9th largest by population.

It has roughly 8.4 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 2.1%, from 146.6 million to 143.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Antarctica, at 14,000,000 km².

It shares land borders with 14 countries: Azerbaijan, Belarus, China, Estonia, Finland, Georgia, Kazakhstan, North Korea, Latvia, Lithuania, Mongolia, Norway, Poland and Ukraine.

Its economic output was about $2.02 trillion in 2023, or $14,055 per person. That ranks 84th of 213 by GDP per person.

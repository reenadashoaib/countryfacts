Belarus in numbers

Belarus is the 87th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 99th largest by population.

It has roughly 44 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 9%, from 10 million to 9.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Guyana, at 214,969 km².

Belarus is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Russia and Ukraine.

Its economic output was about $71.9 billion in 2023, or $7,829 per person. That ranks 111th of 213 by GDP per person.

Montenegro in numbers

Montenegro is the 162nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 169th largest by population.

It has roughly 45 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 3%, from 605 thousand to 623 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Bahamas, at 13,943 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Kosovo and Serbia.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $7.4 billion in 2023, or $11,875 per person. That ranks 92nd of 213 by GDP per person.

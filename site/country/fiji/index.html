Fiji in numbers

Fiji is the 157th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 161st largest by population.

It has roughly 51 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 11%, from 841 thousand to 933 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is New Caledonia, at 18,575 km².

Fiji has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $5.5 billion in 2023, or $5,946 per person. That ranks 127th of 213 by GDP per person.

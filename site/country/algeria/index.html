Algeria in numbers

Algeria is the 11th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 33rd largest by population.

It has roughly 20 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 53.5%, from 30.9 million to 47.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is DR Congo, at 2,344,858 km².

It shares land borders with 7 countries: Tunisia, Libya, Niger, Western Sahara, Mauritania, Mali and Morocco.

The Algerian dinar is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $239.9 billion in 2023, or $5,197 per person. That ranks 132nd of 213 by GDP per person.

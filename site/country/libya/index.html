Libya in numbers

Libya is the 18th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 105th largest by population.

It has roughly 4.2 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 40.6%, from 5.3 million to 7.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Iran, at 1,648,195 km².

It shares land borders with 6 countries: Algeria, Chad, Egypt, Niger, Sudan and Tunisia.

Its economic output was about $50.5 billion in 2023, or $6,911 per person. That ranks 117th of 213 by GDP per person.

Barbados in numbers

Barbados is the 206th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 179th largest by population.

It has roughly 657 people per square kilometre, about 10.9 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 7.8%, from 262 thousand to 283 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Antigua and Barbuda, at 442 km².

Barbados has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $6.4 billion in 2023, or $22,645 per person. That ranks 62nd of 213 by GDP per person.

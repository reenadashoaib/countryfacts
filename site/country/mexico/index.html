Mexico in numbers

Mexico is the 15th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 11th largest by population.

It has roughly 67 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 33.8%, from 98.6 million to 131.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Indonesia, at 1,904,569 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Belize, Guatemala and United States.

Its economic output was about $1.79 trillion in 2023, or $13,788 per person. That ranks 86th of 213 by GDP per person.

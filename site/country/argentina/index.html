Argentina in numbers

Argentina is the 9th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 35th largest by population.

It has roughly 16 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 23.2%, from 37.2 million to 45.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Kazakhstan, at 2,724,900 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Paraguay and Uruguay.

Its economic output was about $640.6 billion in 2023, or $14,067 per person. That ranks 83rd of 213 by GDP per person.

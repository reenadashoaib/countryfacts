Somalia in numbers

Somalia is the 43rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 66th largest by population.

It has roughly 31 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 122.4%, from 8.8 million to 19.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Afghanistan, at 652,230 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Djibouti, Ethiopia and Kenya.

Its economic output was about $11.7 billion in 2023, or $636 per person. That ranks 204th of 213 by GDP per person.

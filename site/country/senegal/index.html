Senegal in numbers

Senegal is the 89th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 68th largest by population.

It has roughly 96 people per square kilometre, about 1.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 89.9%, from 10 million to 18.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Kyrgyzstan, at 199,951 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Gambia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mali and Mauritania.

The West African CFA franc is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $31 billion in 2023, or $1,716 per person. That ranks 174th of 213 by GDP per person.

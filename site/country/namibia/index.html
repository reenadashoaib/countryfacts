Namibia in numbers

Namibia is the 36th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 136th largest by population.

It has roughly 3.7 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 70.1%, from 1.8 million to 3.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Mozambique, at 801,590 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Angola, Botswana, South Africa and Zambia.

The South African rand is also used in 4 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $12.4 billion in 2023, or $4,168 per person. That ranks 141st of 213 by GDP per person.

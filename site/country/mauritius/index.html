Mauritius in numbers

Mauritius is the 182nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 159th largest by population.

It has roughly 610 people per square kilometre, about 10.1 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 4.8%, from 1.2 million to 1.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Comoros, at 1,862 km².

Mauritius has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $14.4 billion in 2023, or $11,530 per person. That ranks 93rd of 213 by GDP per person.

Philippines in numbers

Philippines is the 65th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 14th largest by population.

It has roughly 341 people per square kilometre, about 5.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 46.7%, from 79.6 million to 116.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Congo, at 342,000 km².

Philippines has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $437.1 billion in 2023, or $3,805 per person. That ranks 145th of 213 by GDP per person.

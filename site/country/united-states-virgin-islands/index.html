United States Virgin Islands in numbers

United States Virgin Islands is the 211th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 191st largest by population.

It has roughly 299 people per square kilometre, about 5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 4.5%, from 109 thousand to 104 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Grenada, at 344 km².

United States Virgin Islands has no land borders with other countries.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $4.4 billion in 2021, or $41,976 per person. That ranks 34th of 213 by GDP per person.

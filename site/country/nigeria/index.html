Nigeria in numbers

Nigeria is the 33rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 6th largest by population.

It has roughly 257 people per square kilometre, about 4.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 87.9%, from 126.4 million to 237.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Venezuela, at 916,445 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Benin, Cameroon, Chad and Niger.

Its economic output was about $362.8 billion in 2023, or $1,592 per person. That ranks 180th of 213 by GDP per person.

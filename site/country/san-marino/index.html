San Marino in numbers

San Marino is the 232nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 212th largest by population.

It has roughly 559 people per square kilometre, about 9.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 27.3%, from 27 thousand to 34 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is British Indian Ocean Territory, at 60 km².

San Marino is landlocked and shares land borders with 1 country: Italy.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $1.9 billion in 2021, or $54,169 per person. That ranks 22nd of 213 by GDP per person.

Bahrain in numbers

Bahrain is the 192nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 152nd largest by population.

It has roughly 2,092 people per square kilometre, about 34.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 151%, from 638 thousand to 1.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Dominica, at 751 km².

Bahrain has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $43.2 billion in 2023, or $27,396 per person. That ranks 53rd of 213 by GDP per person.

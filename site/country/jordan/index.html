Jordan in numbers

Jordan is the 113th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 83rd largest by population.

It has roughly 129 people per square kilometre, about 2.1 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 113.9%, from 5.4 million to 11.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Serbia, at 88,361 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Iraq, Israel, Palestine, Saudi Arabia and Syria.

The Jordanian dinar is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $50.8 billion in 2023, or $4,442 per person. That ranks 137th of 213 by GDP per person.

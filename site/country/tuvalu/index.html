Tuvalu in numbers

Tuvalu is the 242nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 216th largest by population.

It has roughly 365 people per square kilometre, about 6 times the world average of 60.

Its population is almost the same as in 2000.

The country closest to it in size is Macau, at 30 km².

Tuvalu has no land borders with other countries.

The Australian dollar is also used in 6 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $62.3 million in 2023, or $6,345 per person. That ranks 122nd of 213 by GDP per person.

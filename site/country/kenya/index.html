Kenya in numbers

Kenya is the 49th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 26th largest by population.

It has roughly 99 people per square kilometre, about 1.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 87.8%, from 30.6 million to 57.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Botswana, at 582,000 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Ethiopia, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania and Uganda.

Its economic output was about $107.4 billion in 2023, or $1,941 per person. That ranks 172nd of 213 by GDP per person.

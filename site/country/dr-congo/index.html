DR Congo in numbers

DR Congo is the 12th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 15th largest by population.

It has roughly 48 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 123.4%, from 50.5 million to 112.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Algeria, at 2,381,741 km².

It shares land borders with 9 countries: Angola, Burundi, Central African Republic, Congo, Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda and Zambia.

Its economic output was about $66.4 billion in 2023, or $628 per person. That ranks 206th of 213 by GDP per person.

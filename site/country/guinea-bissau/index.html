Guinea-Bissau in numbers

Guinea-Bissau is the 139th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 147th largest by population.

It has roughly 62 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 82.2%, from 1.2 million to 2.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Taiwan, at 36,193 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Guinea and Senegal.

The West African CFA franc is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $2 billion in 2023, or $913 per person. That ranks 194th of 213 by GDP per person.

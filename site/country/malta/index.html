Malta in numbers

Malta is the 214th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 170th largest by population.

It has roughly 1,835 people per square kilometre, about 30.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 48.6%, from 390 thousand to 580 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Caribbean Netherlands, at 328 km².

Malta has no land borders with other countries.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $21 billion in 2023, or $37,914 per person. That ranks 38th of 213 by GDP per person.

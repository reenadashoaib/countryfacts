New Caledonia in numbers

New Caledonia is the 156th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 178th largest by population.

It has roughly 16 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 32.4%, from 223 thousand to 295 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Fiji, at 18,272 km².

New Caledonia has no land borders with other countries.

The CFP franc is also used in 2 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $9.6 billion in 2022, or $33,516 per person. That ranks 42nd of 213 by GDP per person.

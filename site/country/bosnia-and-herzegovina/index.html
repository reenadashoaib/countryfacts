Bosnia and Herzegovina in numbers

Bosnia and Herzegovina is the 129th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 135th largest by population.

It has roughly 61 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 24.5%, from 4.2 million to 3.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Costa Rica, at 51,100 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Croatia, Montenegro and Serbia.

Its economic output was about $27.1 billion in 2023, or $8,494 per person. That ranks 103rd of 213 by GDP per person.

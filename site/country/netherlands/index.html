Netherlands in numbers

Netherlands is the 135th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 71st largest by population.

It has roughly 432 people per square kilometre, about 7.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 13.6%, from 15.9 million to 18.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Switzerland, at 41,284 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Belgium and Germany.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $1.12 trillion in 2023, or $62,545 per person. That ranks 18th of 213 by GDP per person.

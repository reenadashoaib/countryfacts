Togo in numbers

Togo is the 127th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 101st largest by population.

It has roughly 151 people per square kilometre, about 2.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 79.6%, from 4.8 million to 8.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Croatia, at 56,594 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Benin, Burkina Faso and Ghana.

The West African CFA franc is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $9.2 billion in 2023, or $1,115 per person. That ranks 190th of 213 by GDP per person.

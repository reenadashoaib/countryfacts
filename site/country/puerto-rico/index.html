Puerto Rico in numbers

Puerto Rico is the 171st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 134th largest by population.

It has roughly 359 people per square kilometre, about 5.9 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 16.4%, from 3.8 million to 3.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Cyprus, at 9,251 km².

Puerto Rico has no land borders with other countries.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $117.9 billion in 2023, or $36,801 per person. That ranks 39th of 213 by GDP per person.

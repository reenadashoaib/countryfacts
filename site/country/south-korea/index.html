South Korea in numbers

South Korea is the 110th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 29th largest by population.

It has roughly 516 people per square kilometre, about 8.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 9.9%, from 47 million to 51.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Iceland, at 103,000 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: North Korea.

Its economic output was about $1.71 trillion in 2023, or $33,121 per person. That ranks 44th of 213 by GDP per person.

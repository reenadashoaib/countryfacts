Portugal in numbers

Portugal is the 112th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 89th largest by population.

It has roughly 117 people per square kilometre, about 1.9 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 5%, from 10.3 million to 10.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Hungary, at 93,028 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Spain.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $287.1 billion in 2023, or $27,139 per person. That ranks 54th of 213 by GDP per person.

Germany in numbers

Germany is the 64th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 19th largest by population.

It has roughly 234 people per square kilometre, about 3.9 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 1.6%, from 82.2 million to 83.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Philippines, at 342,353 km².

It shares land borders with 9 countries: Austria, Belgium, Czechia, Denmark, France, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Poland and Switzerland.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $4.46 trillion in 2023, or $53,503 per person. That ranks 25th of 213 by GDP per person.

Mongolia in numbers

Mongolia is the 20th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 132nd largest by population.

It has roughly 2.3 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 49.4%, from 2.4 million to 3.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Iran, at 1,648,195 km².

Mongolia is landlocked and shares land borders with 2 countries: China and Russia.

Its economic output was about $19.9 billion in 2023, or $5,709 per person. That ranks 128th of 213 by GDP per person.

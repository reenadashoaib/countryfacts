Sierra Leone in numbers

Sierra Leone is the 121st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 100th largest by population.

It has roughly 123 people per square kilometre, about 2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 99.4%, from 4.4 million to 8.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Ireland, at 70,273 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Guinea and Liberia.

Its economic output was about $3.8 billion in 2023, or $450 per person. That ranks 210th of 213 by GDP per person.

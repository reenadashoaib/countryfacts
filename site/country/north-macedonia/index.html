North Macedonia in numbers

North Macedonia is the 150th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 151st largest by population.

It has roughly 71 people per square kilometre, about 1.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 10.1%, from 2 million to 1.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Rwanda, at 26,338 km².

North Macedonia is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: Albania, Bulgaria, Greece, Kosovo and Serbia.

Its economic output was about $14.8 billion in 2023, or $8,076 per person. That ranks 108th of 213 by GDP per person.

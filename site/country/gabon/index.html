Gabon in numbers

Gabon is the 78th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 142nd largest by population.

It has roughly 9.7 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 103.3%, from 1.3 million to 2.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Western Sahara, at 266,000 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Cameroon, Congo and Equatorial Guinea.

The Central African CFA franc is also used in 5 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $20.5 billion in 2023, or $8,257 per person. That ranks 104th of 213 by GDP per person.

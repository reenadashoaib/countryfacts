Slovakia in numbers

Slovakia is the 131st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 122nd largest by population.

It has roughly 110 people per square kilometre, about 1.8 times the world average of 60.

Its population is almost the same as in 2000.

The country closest to it in size is Dominican Republic, at 48,671 km².

Slovakia is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: Austria, Czechia, Hungary, Poland and Ukraine.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $132.8 billion in 2023, or $24,470 per person. That ranks 56th of 213 by GDP per person.

Suriname in numbers

Suriname is the 93rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 168th largest by population.

It has roughly 3.9 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 34.2%, from 477 thousand to 640 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Tunisia, at 163,610 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Brazil, French Guiana and Guyana.

Its economic output was about $3.8 billion in 2023, or $6,015 per person. That ranks 125th of 213 by GDP per person.

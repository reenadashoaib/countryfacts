El Salvador in numbers

El Salvador is the 153rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 112th largest by population.

It has roughly 303 people per square kilometre, about 5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 7.1%, from 5.9 million to 6.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Israel, at 20,770 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Guatemala and Honduras.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $34 billion in 2023, or $5,391 per person. That ranks 131st of 213 by GDP per person.

Saint Lucia in numbers

Saint Lucia is the 197th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 183rd largest by population.

It has roughly 292 people per square kilometre, about 4.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 13.6%, from 159 thousand to 180 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Isle of Man, at 572 km².

Saint Lucia has no land borders with other countries.

The Eastern Caribbean dollar is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $2.5 billion in 2023, or $14,055 per person. That ranks 85th of 213 by GDP per person.

Eritrea in numbers

Eritrea is the 102nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 131st largest by population.

It has roughly 31 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 60.5%, from 2.2 million to 3.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Malawi, at 118,484 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Djibouti, Ethiopia and Sudan.

Its economic output was about $2.1 billion in 2011, or $689 per person. That ranks 200th of 213 by GDP per person.

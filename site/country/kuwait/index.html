Kuwait in numbers

Kuwait is the 158th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 127th largest by population.

It has roughly 273 people per square kilometre, about 4.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 148.9%, from 2 million to 4.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Fiji, at 18,272 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Iraq and Saudi Arabia.

Its economic output was about $161.8 billion in 2023, or $33,332 per person. That ranks 43rd of 213 by GDP per person.

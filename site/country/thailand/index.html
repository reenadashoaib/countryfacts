Thailand in numbers

Thailand is the 52nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 20th largest by population.

It has roughly 140 people per square kilometre, about 2.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 13.7%, from 63 million to 71.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Spain, at 505,992 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Myanmar, Cambodia, Laos and Malaysia.

Its economic output was about $514.9 billion in 2023, or $7,182 per person. That ranks 114th of 213 by GDP per person.

Benin in numbers

Benin is the 103rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 75th largest by population.

It has roughly 132 people per square kilometre, about 2.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 105.1%, from 7.2 million to 14.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Honduras, at 112,492 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Burkina Faso, Niger, Nigeria and Togo.

The West African CFA franc is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $19.7 billion in 2023, or $1,394 per person. That ranks 182nd of 213 by GDP per person.

Hong Kong in numbers

Hong Kong is the 188th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 104th largest by population.

It has roughly 6,792 people per square kilometre, about 112.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 12.5%, from 6.7 million to 7.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Martinique, at 1,128 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: China.

Its economic output was about $382.1 billion in 2023, or $50,697 per person. That ranks 28th of 213 by GDP per person.

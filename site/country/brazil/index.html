Brazil in numbers

Brazil is the 6th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 7th largest by population.

It has roughly 25 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 22.3%, from 174 million to 212.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is United States, at 9,372,610 km².

It shares land borders with 10 countries: Argentina, Bolivia, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay and Venezuela.

Its economic output was about $2.17 trillion in 2023, or $10,295 per person. That ranks 99th of 213 by GDP per person.

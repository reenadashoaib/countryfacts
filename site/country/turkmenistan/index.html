Turkmenistan in numbers

Turkmenistan is the 54th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 103rd largest by population.

It has roughly 16 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 66.3%, from 4.6 million to 7.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Cameroon, at 475,442 km².

Turkmenistan is landlocked and shares land borders with 4 countries: Afghanistan, Iran, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan.

Its economic output was about $59.9 billion in 2023, or $8,132 per person. That ranks 107th of 213 by GDP per person.

Zimbabwe in numbers

Zimbabwe is the 62nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 73rd largest by population.

It has roughly 43 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 42.5%, from 11.9 million to 17 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Japan, at 377,930 km².

Zimbabwe is landlocked and shares land borders with 4 countries: Botswana, Mozambique, South Africa and Zambia.

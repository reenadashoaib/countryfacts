Gambia in numbers

Gambia is the 168th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 141st largest by population.

It has roughly 264 people per square kilometre, about 4.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 93.9%, from 1.5 million to 2.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Kosovo, at 10,908 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Senegal.

Its economic output was about $2.3 billion in 2023, or $867 per person. That ranks 198th of 213 by GDP per person.

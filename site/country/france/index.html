France in numbers

France is the 50th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 23rd largest by population.

It has roughly 125 people per square kilometre, about 2.1 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 12.8%, from 60.9 million to 68.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Yemen, at 527,968 km².

It shares land borders with 8 countries: Andorra, Belgium, Germany, Italy, Luxembourg, Monaco, Spain and Switzerland.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $3.03 trillion in 2023, or $44,329 per person. That ranks 33rd of 213 by GDP per person.

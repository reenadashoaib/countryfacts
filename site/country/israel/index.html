Israel in numbers

Israel is the 154th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 95th largest by population.

It has roughly 487 people per square kilometre, about 8.1 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 61%, from 6.3 million to 10.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is El Salvador, at 21,041 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Palestine and Syria.

The Israeli new shekel is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $509.9 billion in 2023, or $51,772 per person. That ranks 27th of 213 by GDP per person.

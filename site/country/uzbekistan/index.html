Uzbekistan in numbers

Uzbekistan is the 58th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 42nd largest by population.

It has roughly 83 people per square kilometre, about 1.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 49.5%, from 24.8 million to 37.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Morocco, at 446,550 km².

Uzbekistan is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: Afghanistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Turkmenistan.

Its economic output was about $90.9 billion in 2023, or $2,549 per person. That ranks 160th of 213 by GDP per person.

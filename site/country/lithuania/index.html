Lithuania in numbers

Lithuania is the 125th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 139th largest by population.

It has roughly 44 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 17.5%, from 3.5 million to 2.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Sri Lanka, at 65,610 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Belarus, Latvia, Poland and Russia.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $77.8 billion in 2023, or $27,106 per person. That ranks 55th of 213 by GDP per person.

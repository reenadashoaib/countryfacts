Eswatini in numbers

Eswatini is the 159th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 158th largest by population.

It has roughly 72 people per square kilometre, about 1.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 20.6%, from 1 million to 1.3 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Kuwait, at 17,818 km².

Eswatini is landlocked and shares land borders with 2 countries: Mozambique and South Africa.

The South African rand is also used in 4 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $4.6 billion in 2023, or $3,737 per person. That ranks 148th of 213 by GDP per person.

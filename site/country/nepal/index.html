Nepal in numbers

Nepal is the 96th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 52nd largest by population.

It has roughly 201 people per square kilometre, about 3.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 20.7%, from 24.5 million to 29.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Bangladesh, at 147,570 km².

Nepal is landlocked and shares land borders with 2 countries: China and India.

Its economic output was about $40.9 billion in 2023, or $1,378 per person. That ranks 183rd of 213 by GDP per person.

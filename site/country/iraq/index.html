Iraq in numbers

Iraq is the 60th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 34th largest by population.

It has roughly 107 people per square kilometre, about 1.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 92.5%, from 24.4 million to 47 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Morocco, at 446,550 km².

It shares land borders with 6 countries: Iran, Jordan, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Syria and Türkiye.

Its economic output was about $250.8 billion in 2023, or $5,565 per person. That ranks 130th of 213 by GDP per person.

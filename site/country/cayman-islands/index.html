Cayman Islands in numbers

Cayman Islands is the 216th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 197th largest by population.

It has roughly 287 people per square kilometre, about 4.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 91.2%, from 40 thousand to 76 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Saint Kitts and Nevis, at 261 km².

Cayman Islands has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $6.6 billion in 2022, or $92,202 per person. That ranks 8th of 213 by GDP per person.

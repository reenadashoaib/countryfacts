Bahamas in numbers

Bahamas is the 161st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 175th largest by population.

It has roughly 29 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 24.5%, from 324 thousand to 403 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Montenegro, at 13,812 km².

Bahamas has no land borders with other countries.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $14.3 billion in 2023, or $35,897 per person. That ranks 40th of 213 by GDP per person.

Djibouti in numbers

Djibouti is the 151st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 160th largest by population.

It has roughly 51 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 58.4%, from 747 thousand to 1.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Belize, at 22,966 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Eritrea, Ethiopia and Somalia.

Its economic output was about $4.1 billion in 2023, or $3,555 per person. That ranks 151st of 213 by GDP per person.

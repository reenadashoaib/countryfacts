Ethiopia in numbers

Ethiopia is the 28th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 10th largest by population.

It has roughly 123 people per square kilometre, about 2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 101%, from 67.4 million to 135.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Bolivia, at 1,098,581 km².

Ethiopia is landlocked and shares land borders with 6 countries: Djibouti, Eritrea, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan and Sudan.

Its economic output was about $163.7 billion in 2023, or $1,272 per person. That ranks 186th of 213 by GDP per person.

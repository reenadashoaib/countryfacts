Peru in numbers

Peru is the 21st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 48th largest by population.

It has roughly 27 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 30%, from 26.6 million to 34.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Chad, at 1,284,000 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia and Ecuador.

Its economic output was about $267.6 billion in 2023, or $7,907 per person. That ranks 110th of 213 by GDP per person.

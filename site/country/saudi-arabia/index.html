Saudi Arabia in numbers

Saudi Arabia is the 14th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 43rd largest by population.

It has roughly 17 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 128.5%, from 16.2 million to 37 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Greenland, at 2,166,086 km².

It shares land borders with 7 countries: Iraq, Jordan, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, United Arab Emirates and Yemen.

Its economic output was about $1.07 trillion in 2023, or $31,676 per person. That ranks 49th of 213 by GDP per person.

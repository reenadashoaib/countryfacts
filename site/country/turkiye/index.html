Türkiye in numbers

Türkiye is the 38th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 18th largest by population.

It has roughly 110 people per square kilometre, about 1.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 31.3%, from 65.4 million to 85.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Mozambique, at 801,590 km².

It shares land borders with 8 countries: Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bulgaria, Georgia, Greece, Iran, Iraq and Syria.

Its economic output was about $1.11 trillion in 2023, or $12,986 per person. That ranks 87th of 213 by GDP per person.

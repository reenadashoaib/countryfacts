Sri Lanka in numbers

Sri Lanka is the 124th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 62nd largest by population.

It has roughly 332 people per square kilometre, about 5.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 12.8%, from 19.3 million to 21.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Lithuania, at 65,300 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: India.

Its economic output was about $84.4 billion in 2023, or $3,828 per person. That ranks 144th of 213 by GDP per person.

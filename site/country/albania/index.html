Albania in numbers

Albania is the 145th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 146th largest by population.

It has roughly 82 people per square kilometre, about 1.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 23.9%, from 3.1 million to 2.3 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Solomon Islands, at 28,896 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Montenegro, Greece, North Macedonia and Kosovo.

Its economic output was about $23 billion in 2023, or $9,528 per person. That ranks 102nd of 213 by GDP per person.

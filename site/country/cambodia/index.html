Cambodia in numbers

Cambodia is the 91st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 72nd largest by population.

It has roughly 99 people per square kilometre, about 1.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 43.2%, from 12.5 million to 17.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Uruguay, at 181,034 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Laos, Thailand and Vietnam.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $31.8 billion in 2023, or $1,824 per person. That ranks 173rd of 213 by GDP per person.

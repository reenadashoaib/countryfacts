Guatemala in numbers

Guatemala is the 108th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 69th largest by population.

It has roughly 172 people per square kilometre, about 2.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 59.8%, from 11.7 million to 18.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Cuba, at 109,884 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Belize, El Salvador, Honduras and Mexico.

Its economic output was about $102.1 billion in 2023, or $5,630 per person. That ranks 129th of 213 by GDP per person.

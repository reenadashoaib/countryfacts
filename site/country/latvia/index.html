Latvia in numbers

Latvia is the 126th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 150th largest by population.

It has roughly 29 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 22%, from 2.4 million to 1.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Lithuania, at 65,300 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Belarus, Estonia, Lithuania and Russia.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $43.6 billion in 2023, or $23,160 per person. That ranks 58th of 213 by GDP per person.

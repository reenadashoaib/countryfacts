Australia in numbers

Australia is the 7th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 55th largest by population.

It has roughly 3.6 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 45.1%, from 19 million to 27.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Brazil, at 8,515,767 km².

Australia has no land borders with other countries.

The Australian dollar is also used in 6 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $1.72 trillion in 2023, or $64,660 per person. That ranks 17th of 213 by GDP per person.

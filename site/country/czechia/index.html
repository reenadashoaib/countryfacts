Czechia in numbers

Czechia is the 119th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 88th largest by population.

It has roughly 138 people per square kilometre, about 2.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 6.2%, from 10.3 million to 10.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Panama, at 75,417 km².

Czechia is landlocked and shares land borders with 4 countries: Austria, Germany, Poland and Slovakia.

Its economic output was about $330.9 billion in 2023, or $30,454 per person. That ranks 50th of 213 by GDP per person.

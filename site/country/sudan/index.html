Sudan in numbers

Sudan is the 17th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 30th largest by population.

It has roughly 27 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 85.7%, from 27.8 million to 51.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Indonesia, at 1,904,569 km².

It shares land borders with 7 countries: Central African Republic, Chad, Egypt, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Libya and South Sudan.

Its economic output was about $109.3 billion in 2023, or $2,185 per person. That ranks 166th of 213 by GDP per person.

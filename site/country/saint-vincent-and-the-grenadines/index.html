Saint Vincent and the Grenadines in numbers

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is the 209th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 193rd largest by population.

It has roughly 257 people per square kilometre, about 4.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 12%, from 114 thousand to 100 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha, at 394 km².

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines has no land borders with other countries.

The Eastern Caribbean dollar is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $1.1 billion in 2023, or $10,520 per person. That ranks 98th of 213 by GDP per person.

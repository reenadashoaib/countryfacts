Finland in numbers

Finland is the 67th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 117th largest by population.

It has roughly 17 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 9.1%, from 5.2 million to 5.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Congo, at 342,000 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Norway, Sweden and Russia.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $300.2 billion in 2023, or $53,759 per person. That ranks 23rd of 213 by GDP per person.

Haiti in numbers

Haiti is the 148th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 82nd largest by population.

It has roughly 429 people per square kilometre, about 7.1 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 43.4%, from 8.3 million to 11.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Burundi, at 27,834 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Dominican Republic.

Its economic output was about $19.9 billion in 2023, or $1,706 per person. That ranks 175th of 213 by GDP per person.

Norway in numbers

Norway is the 70th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 118th largest by population.

It has roughly 17 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 24.9%, from 4.5 million to 5.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Ivory Coast, at 322,463 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Finland, Sweden and Russia.

The Norwegian krone is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $485.5 billion in 2023, or $87,962 per person. That ranks 10th of 213 by GDP per person.

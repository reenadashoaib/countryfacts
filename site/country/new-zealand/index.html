New Zealand in numbers

New Zealand is the 77th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 124th largest by population.

It has roughly 20 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 38%, from 3.9 million to 5.3 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Burkina Faso, at 272,967 km².

New Zealand has no land borders with other countries.

The New Zealand dollar is also used in 4 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $253.5 billion in 2023, or $48,743 per person. That ranks 29th of 213 by GDP per person.

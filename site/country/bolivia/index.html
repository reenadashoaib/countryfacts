Bolivia in numbers

Bolivia is the 29th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 78th largest by population.

It has roughly 11 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 46.2%, from 8.6 million to 12.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Ethiopia, at 1,104,300 km².

Bolivia is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Paraguay and Peru.

Its economic output was about $45.8 billion in 2023, or $3,745 per person. That ranks 146th of 213 by GDP per person.

Tonga in numbers

Tonga is the 194th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 192nd largest by population.

It has roughly 139 people per square kilometre, about 2.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 1%, from 103 thousand to 104 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Dominica, at 751 km².

Tonga has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $500.3 million in 2022, or $4,763 per person. That ranks 135th of 213 by GDP per person.

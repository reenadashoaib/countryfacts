Liechtenstein in numbers

Liechtenstein is the 224th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 207th largest by population.

It has roughly 256 people per square kilometre, about 4.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 25.7%, from 33 thousand to 41 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is British Virgin Islands, at 151 km².

Liechtenstein is landlocked and shares land borders with 2 countries: Austria and Switzerland.

The Swiss franc is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $7.4 billion in 2022, or $186,480 per person. That ranks 2nd of 213 by GDP per person.

Tanzania in numbers

Tanzania is the 32nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 21st largest by population.

It has roughly 75 people per square kilometre, about 1.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 105.9%, from 34.3 million to 70.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Nigeria, at 923,768 km².

It shares land borders with 8 countries: Burundi, DR Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Rwanda, Uganda and Zambia.

Its economic output was about $79.2 billion in 2023, or $1,188 per person. That ranks 188th of 213 by GDP per person.

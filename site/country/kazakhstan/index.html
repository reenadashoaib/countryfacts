Kazakhstan in numbers

Kazakhstan is the 10th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 64th largest by population.

It has roughly 7.6 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 34.5%, from 15.5 million to 20.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Argentina, at 2,780,400 km².

Kazakhstan is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: China, Kyrgyzstan, Russia, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan.

Its economic output was about $261.4 billion in 2023, or $12,859 per person. That ranks 89th of 213 by GDP per person.

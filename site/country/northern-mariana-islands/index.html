Northern Mariana Islands in numbers

Northern Mariana Islands is the 201st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 206th largest by population.

It has roughly 94 people per square kilometre, about 1.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 36.3%, from 68 thousand to 44 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Andorra, at 468 km².

Northern Mariana Islands has no land borders with other countries.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $858 million in 2020, or $18,053 per person. That ranks 75th of 213 by GDP per person.

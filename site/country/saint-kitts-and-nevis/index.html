Saint Kitts and Nevis in numbers

Saint Kitts and Nevis is the 217th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 202nd largest by population.

It has roughly 180 people per square kilometre, about 3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 3.7%, from 45 thousand to 47 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Niue, at 260 km².

Saint Kitts and Nevis has no land borders with other countries.

The Eastern Caribbean dollar is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $1.1 billion in 2023, or $23,034 per person. That ranks 59th of 213 by GDP per person.

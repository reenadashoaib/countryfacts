Slovenia in numbers

Slovenia is the 155th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 148th largest by population.

It has roughly 105 people per square kilometre, about 1.7 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 7.1%, from 2 million to 2.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Israel, at 20,770 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Austria, Croatia, Italy and Hungary.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $68.2 billion in 2023, or $32,171 per person. That ranks 48th of 213 by GDP per person.

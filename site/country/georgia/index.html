Georgia in numbers

Georgia is the 123rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 129th largest by population.

It has roughly 56 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 3.5%, from 4.1 million to 3.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Ireland, at 70,273 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Armenia, Azerbaijan, Russia and Türkiye.

Its economic output was about $30.5 billion in 2023, or $8,218 per person. That ranks 105th of 213 by GDP per person.

Samoa in numbers

Samoa is the 179th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 182nd largest by population.

It has roughly 77 people per square kilometre, about 1.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 20.7%, from 182 thousand to 219 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Luxembourg, at 2,586 km².

Samoa has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $934.1 million in 2023, or $4,311 per person. That ranks 139th of 213 by GDP per person.

Spain in numbers

Spain is the 53rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 32nd largest by population.

It has roughly 98 people per square kilometre, about 1.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 21.7%, from 40.6 million to 49.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Thailand, at 513,120 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Andorra, France, Gibraltar, Portugal and Morocco.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $1.58 trillion in 2023, or $32,691 per person. That ranks 47th of 213 by GDP per person.

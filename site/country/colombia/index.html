Colombia in numbers

Colombia is the 27th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 28th largest by population.

It has roughly 47 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 36.7%, from 39.1 million to 53.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Ethiopia, at 1,104,300 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Brazil, Ecuador, Panama, Peru and Venezuela.

Its economic output was about $363.5 billion in 2023, or $6,948 per person. That ranks 116th of 213 by GDP per person.

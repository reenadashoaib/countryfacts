Seychelles in numbers

Seychelles is the 203rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 187th largest by population.

It has roughly 272 people per square kilometre, about 4.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 51.3%, from 81 thousand to 123 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Palau, at 459 km².

Seychelles has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $2.1 billion in 2023, or $17,879 per person. That ranks 77th of 213 by GDP per person.

Belize in numbers

Belize is the 152nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 174th largest by population.

It has roughly 18 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 75.6%, from 241 thousand to 423 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Djibouti, at 23,200 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Guatemala and Mexico.

Its economic output was about $3.3 billion in 2023, or $7,982 per person. That ranks 109th of 213 by GDP per person.

Panama in numbers

Panama is the 120th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 128th largest by population.

It has roughly 61 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 51.8%, from 3 million to 4.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Czechia, at 78,865 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Colombia and Costa Rica.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $83.4 billion in 2023, or $18,701 per person. That ranks 73rd of 213 by GDP per person.

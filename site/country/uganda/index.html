Uganda in numbers

Uganda is the 82nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 31st largest by population.

It has roughly 213 people per square kilometre, about 3.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 114.1%, from 24 million to 51.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is United Kingdom, at 242,900 km².

Uganda is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: DR Congo, Kenya, Rwanda, South Sudan and Tanzania.

Its economic output was about $49.3 billion in 2023, or $1,013 per person. That ranks 192nd of 213 by GDP per person.

American Samoa in numbers

American Samoa is the 221st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 204th largest by population.

It has roughly 231 people per square kilometre, about 3.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 19%, from 57 thousand to 46 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Marshall Islands, at 181 km².

American Samoa has no land borders with other countries.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $871 million in 2022, or $18,017 per person. That ranks 76th of 213 by GDP per person.

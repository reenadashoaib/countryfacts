Italy in numbers

Italy is the 74th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 25th largest by population.

It has roughly 196 people per square kilometre, about 3.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 3.5%, from 56.9 million to 58.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Oman, at 309,500 km².

It shares land borders with 6 countries: Austria, France, San Marino, Slovenia, Switzerland and Vatican City.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $2.25 trillion in 2023, or $38,228 per person. That ranks 36th of 213 by GDP per person.

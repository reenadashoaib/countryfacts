Uruguay in numbers

Uruguay is the 92nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 133rd largest by population.

It has roughly 19 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 3.6%, from 3.3 million to 3.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Cambodia, at 181,035 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Argentina and Brazil.

Its economic output was about $77.2 billion in 2023, or $22,798 per person. That ranks 61st of 213 by GDP per person.

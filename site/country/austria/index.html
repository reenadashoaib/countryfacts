Austria in numbers

Austria is the 116th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 97th largest by population.

It has roughly 110 people per square kilometre, about 1.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 14.9%, from 8 million to 9.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is United Arab Emirates, at 83,600 km².

Austria is landlocked and shares land borders with 8 countries: Czechia, Germany, Hungary, Italy, Liechtenstein, Slovakia, Slovenia and Switzerland.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $516 billion in 2023, or $56,510 per person. That ranks 20th of 213 by GDP per person.

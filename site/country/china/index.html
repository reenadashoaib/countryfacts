China in numbers

China is the 4th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 2nd largest by population.

It has roughly 145 people per square kilometre, about 2.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 11.4%, from 1.3 billion to 1.4 billion in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Canada, at 9,984,670 km².

It shares land borders with 16 countries: Afghanistan, Bhutan, Myanmar, Hong Kong, India, Kazakhstan, Nepal, North Korea, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Macau, Mongolia, Pakistan, Russia, Tajikistan and Vietnam.

The Chinese yuan is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $17.79 trillion in 2023, or $12,614 per person. That ranks 90th of 213 by GDP per person.

Armenia in numbers

Armenia is the 143rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 137th largest by population.

It has roughly 104 people per square kilometre, about 1.7 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 4.2%, from 3.2 million to 3.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Lesotho, at 30,355 km².

Armenia is landlocked and shares land borders with 4 countries: Azerbaijan, Georgia, Iran and Türkiye.

Its economic output was about $24.2 billion in 2023, or $8,168 per person. That ranks 106th of 213 by GDP per person.

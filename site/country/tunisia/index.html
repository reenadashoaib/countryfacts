Tunisia in numbers

Tunisia is the 94th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 79th largest by population.

It has roughly 75 people per square kilometre, about 1.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 26.5%, from 9.8 million to 12.3 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Suriname, at 163,820 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Algeria and Libya.

Its economic output was about $48.5 billion in 2023, or $3,978 per person. That ranks 143rd of 213 by GDP per person.

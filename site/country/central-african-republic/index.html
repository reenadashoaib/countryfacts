Central African Republic in numbers

Central African Republic is the 44th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 119th largest by population.

It has roughly 8.8 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 43.8%, from 3.8 million to 5.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is South Sudan, at 619,745 km².

Central African Republic is landlocked and shares land borders with 6 countries: Cameroon, Chad, DR Congo, Congo, South Sudan and Sudan.

The Central African CFA franc is also used in 5 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $2.6 billion in 2023, or $496 per person. That ranks 209th of 213 by GDP per person.

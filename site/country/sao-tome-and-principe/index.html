São Tomé and Príncipe in numbers

São Tomé and Príncipe is the 189th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 181st largest by population.

It has roughly 249 people per square kilometre, about 4.1 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 66.8%, from 144 thousand to 240 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Turks and Caicos Islands, at 948 km².

São Tomé and Príncipe has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $603.2 million in 2023, or $2,613 per person. That ranks 158th of 213 by GDP per person.

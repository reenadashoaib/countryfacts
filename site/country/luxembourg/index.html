Luxembourg in numbers

Luxembourg is the 180th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 166th largest by population.

It has roughly 266 people per square kilometre, about 4.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 57.5%, from 436 thousand to 687 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Réunion, at 2,511 km².

Luxembourg is landlocked and shares land borders with 3 countries: Belgium, France and Germany.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $85.8 billion in 2023, or $128,678 per person. That ranks 3rd of 213 by GDP per person.

Marshall Islands in numbers

Marshall Islands is the 222nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 211th largest by population.

It has roughly 200 people per square kilometre, about 3.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 28.4%, from 51 thousand to 36 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Aruba, at 180 km².

Marshall Islands has no land borders with other countries.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $284 million in 2023, or $7,314 per person. That ranks 113th of 213 by GDP per person.

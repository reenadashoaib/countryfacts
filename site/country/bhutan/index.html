Bhutan in numbers

Bhutan is the 137th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 165th largest by population.

It has roughly 21 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 33.5%, from 597 thousand to 797 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Taiwan, at 36,193 km².

Bhutan is landlocked and shares land borders with 2 countries: China and India.

The Indian rupee is also used in 2 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $2.9 billion in 2022, or $3,711 per person. That ranks 149th of 213 by GDP per person.

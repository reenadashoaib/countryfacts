Honduras in numbers

Honduras is the 104th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 86th largest by population.

It has roughly 98 people per square kilometre, about 1.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 67.3%, from 6.6 million to 11 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Benin, at 112,622 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Guatemala, El Salvador and Nicaragua.

Its economic output was about $34.4 billion in 2023, or $3,232 per person. That ranks 155th of 213 by GDP per person.

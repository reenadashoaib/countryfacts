Congo in numbers

Congo is the 66th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 110th largest by population.

It has roughly 19 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 105.8%, from 3.2 million to 6.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Philippines, at 342,353 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, DR Congo and Gabon.

The Central African CFA franc is also used in 5 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $15.3 billion in 2023, or $2,478 per person. That ranks 162nd of 213 by GDP per person.

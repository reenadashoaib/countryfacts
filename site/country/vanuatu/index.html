Vanuatu in numbers

Vanuatu is the 163rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 177th largest by population.

It has roughly 27 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 79.9%, from 186 thousand to 335 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Falkland Islands, at 12,173 km².

Vanuatu has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $1.1 billion in 2023, or $3,515 per person. That ranks 152nd of 213 by GDP per person.

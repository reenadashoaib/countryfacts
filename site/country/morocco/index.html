Morocco in numbers

Morocco is the 59th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 41st largest by population.

It has roughly 86 people per square kilometre, about 1.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 35.2%, from 28.4 million to 38.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Uzbekistan, at 447,400 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Algeria, Western Sahara and Spain.

The Moroccan dirham is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $141.1 billion in 2023, or $3,742 per person. That ranks 147th of 213 by GDP per person.

Sweden in numbers

Sweden is the 57th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 92nd largest by population.

It has roughly 24 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 19.4%, from 8.9 million to 10.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Uzbekistan, at 447,400 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Finland and Norway.

Its economic output was about $593.3 billion in 2023, or $56,305 per person. That ranks 21st of 213 by GDP per person.

Serbia in numbers

Serbia is the 114th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 109th largest by population.

It has roughly 74 people per square kilometre, about 1.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 12.9%, from 7.5 million to 6.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Jordan, at 89,342 km².

Serbia is landlocked and shares land borders with 8 countries: Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Hungary, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Montenegro and Romania.

Its economic output was about $75.2 billion in 2023, or $11,352 per person. That ranks 95th of 213 by GDP per person.

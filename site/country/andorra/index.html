Andorra in numbers

Andorra is the 200th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 196th largest by population.

It has roughly 177 people per square kilometre, about 2.9 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 26.2%, from 66 thousand to 83 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Northern Mariana Islands, at 464 km².

Andorra is landlocked and shares land borders with 2 countries: France and Spain.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $3.7 billion in 2023, or $46,103 per person. That ranks 32nd of 213 by GDP per person.

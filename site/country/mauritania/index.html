Mauritania in numbers

Mauritania is the 30th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 125th largest by population.

It has roughly 5.2 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 103.4%, from 2.6 million to 5.3 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Egypt, at 1,002,450 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Algeria, Mali, Senegal and Western Sahara.

The Mauritanian ouguiya is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $10.5 billion in 2023, or $2,081 per person. That ranks 168th of 213 by GDP per person.

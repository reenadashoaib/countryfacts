Iceland in numbers

Iceland is the 109th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 176th largest by population.

It has roughly 3.8 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 39.5%, from 281 thousand to 392 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is South Korea, at 100,210 km².

Iceland has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $31 billion in 2023, or $80,433 per person. That ranks 13th of 213 by GDP per person.

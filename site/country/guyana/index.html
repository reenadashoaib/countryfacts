Guyana in numbers

Guyana is the 86th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 164th largest by population.

It has roughly 3.9 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 9.3%, from 765 thousand to 836 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Belarus, at 207,600 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Brazil, Suriname and Venezuela.

Its economic output was about $16.8 billion in 2023, or $20,314 per person. That ranks 71st of 213 by GDP per person.

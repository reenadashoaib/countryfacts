Zambia in numbers

Zambia is the 40th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 61st largest by population.

It has roughly 29 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 118.8%, from 10 million to 21.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Chile, at 756,102 km².

Zambia is landlocked and shares land borders with 8 countries: Angola, Botswana, DR Congo, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia, Tanzania and Zimbabwe.

Its economic output was about $28.2 billion in 2023, or $1,359 per person. That ranks 185th of 213 by GDP per person.

Kyrgyzstan in numbers

Kyrgyzstan is the 88th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 106th largest by population.

It has roughly 37 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 49.9%, from 4.9 million to 7.3 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Senegal, at 196,722 km².

Kyrgyzstan is landlocked and shares land borders with 4 countries: China, Kazakhstan, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan.

Its economic output was about $14 billion in 2023, or $1,970 per person. That ranks 171st of 213 by GDP per person.

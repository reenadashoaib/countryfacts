Romania in numbers

Romania is the 84th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 67th largest by population.

It has roughly 80 people per square kilometre, about 1.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 15.3%, from 22.4 million to 19 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Ghana, at 238,533 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Bulgaria, Hungary, Moldova, Serbia and Ukraine.

Its economic output was about $351 billion in 2023, or $18,415 per person. That ranks 74th of 213 by GDP per person.

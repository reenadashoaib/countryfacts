Costa Rica in numbers

Costa Rica is the 130th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 126th largest by population.

It has roughly 101 people per square kilometre, about 1.7 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 30.9%, from 3.9 million to 5.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Bosnia and Herzegovina, at 51,209 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Nicaragua and Panama.

Its economic output was about $86.5 billion in 2023, or $16,942 per person. That ranks 79th of 213 by GDP per person.

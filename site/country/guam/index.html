Guam in numbers

Guam is the 199th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 184th largest by population.

It has roughly 308 people per square kilometre, about 5.1 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 5.6%, from 160 thousand to 169 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Isle of Man, at 572 km².

Guam has no land borders with other countries.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $6.9 billion in 2022, or $41,833 per person. That ranks 35th of 213 by GDP per person.

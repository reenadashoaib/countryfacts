Dominica in numbers

Dominica is the 193rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 198th largest by population.

It has roughly 88 people per square kilometre, about 1.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 3.9%, from 69 thousand to 66 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Tonga, at 747 km².

Dominica has no land borders with other countries.

The Eastern Caribbean dollar is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $654 million in 2023, or $9,833 per person. That ranks 100th of 213 by GDP per person.

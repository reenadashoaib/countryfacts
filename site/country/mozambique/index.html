Mozambique in numbers

Mozambique is the 37th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 46th largest by population.

It has roughly 44 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 96.5%, from 18.1 million to 35.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Türkiye, at 783,562 km².

It shares land borders with 6 countries: Malawi, South Africa, Eswatini, Tanzania, Zambia and Zimbabwe.

Its economic output was about $20.6 billion in 2023, or $613 per person. That ranks 207th of 213 by GDP per person.

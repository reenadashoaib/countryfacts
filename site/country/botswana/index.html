Botswana in numbers

Botswana is the 48th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 143rd largest by population.

It has roughly 4.4 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 52.7%, from 1.7 million to 2.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Kenya, at 580,367 km².

Botswana is landlocked and shares land borders with 4 countries: Namibia, South Africa, Zambia and Zimbabwe.

The Botswana pula is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $19.4 billion in 2023, or $7,820 per person. That ranks 112th of 213 by GDP per person.

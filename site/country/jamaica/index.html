Jamaica in numbers

Jamaica is the 166th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 140th largest by population.

It has roughly 258 people per square kilometre, about 4.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 8.8%, from 2.6 million to 2.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Kosovo, at 10,908 km².

Jamaica has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $19.4 billion in 2023, or $6,840 per person. That ranks 118th of 213 by GDP per person.

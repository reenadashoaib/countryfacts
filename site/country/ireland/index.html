Ireland in numbers

Ireland is the 122nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 121st largest by population.

It has roughly 78 people per square kilometre, about 1.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 44.1%, from 3.8 million to 5.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Georgia, at 69,700 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: United Kingdom.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $545.6 billion in 2023, or $102,725 per person. That ranks 5th of 213 by GDP per person.

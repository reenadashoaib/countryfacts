United Arab Emirates in numbers

United Arab Emirates is the 117th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 85th largest by population.

It has roughly 138 people per square kilometre, about 2.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 229.6%, from 3.5 million to 11.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is French Guiana, at 83,534 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Oman and Saudi Arabia.

Its economic output was about $504.2 billion in 2023, or $48,091 per person. That ranks 31st of 213 by GDP per person.

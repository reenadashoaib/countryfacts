Afghanistan in numbers

Afghanistan is the 42nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 36th largest by population.

It has roughly 67 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 117.8%, from 20.1 million to 43.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Somalia, at 637,657 km².

Afghanistan is landlocked and shares land borders with 6 countries: Iran, Pakistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and China.

Its economic output was about $14.5 billion in 2022, or $357 per person. That ranks 212th of 213 by GDP per person.

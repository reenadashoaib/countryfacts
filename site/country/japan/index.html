Japan in numbers

Japan is the 63rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 12th largest by population.

It has roughly 326 people per square kilometre, about 5.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 2.7%, from 126.8 million to 123.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Zimbabwe, at 390,757 km².

Japan has no land borders with other countries.

The Japanese yen is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $4.21 trillion in 2023, or $33,834 per person. That ranks 41st of 213 by GDP per person.

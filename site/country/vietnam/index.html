Vietnam in numbers

Vietnam is the 68th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 16th largest by population.

It has roughly 307 people per square kilometre, about 5.1 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 31.7%, from 77.2 million to 101.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Malaysia, at 330,803 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Cambodia, China and Laos.

Its economic output was about $429.7 billion in 2023, or $4,282 per person. That ranks 140th of 213 by GDP per person.

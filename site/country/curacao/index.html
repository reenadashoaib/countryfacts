Curaçao in numbers

Curaçao is the 204th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 185th largest by population.

It has roughly 352 people per square kilometre, about 5.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 16.7%, from 134 thousand to 156 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Antigua and Barbuda, at 442 km².

Curaçao has no land borders with other countries.

The Caribbean guilder is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $3.1 billion in 2022, or $20,048 per person. That ranks 72nd of 213 by GDP per person.

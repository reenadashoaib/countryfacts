Switzerland in numbers

Switzerland is the 136th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 98th largest by population.

It has roughly 220 people per square kilometre, about 3.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 26.6%, from 7.2 million to 9.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Netherlands, at 41,850 km².

Switzerland is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: Austria, France, Italy, Liechtenstein and Germany.

The Swiss franc is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $884.9 billion in 2023, or $99,557 per person. That ranks 6th of 213 by GDP per person.

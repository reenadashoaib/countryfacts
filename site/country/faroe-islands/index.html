Faroe Islands in numbers

Faroe Islands is the 186th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 201st largest by population.

It has roughly 39 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 20%, from 46 thousand to 55 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Åland Islands, at 1,580 km².

Faroe Islands has no land borders with other countries.

The Danish krone is also used in 2 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $3.6 billion in 2022, or $65,969 per person. That ranks 16th of 213 by GDP per person.

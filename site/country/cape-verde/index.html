Cape Verde in numbers

Cape Verde is the 177th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 172nd largest by population.

It has roughly 131 people per square kilometre, about 2.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 16.3%, from 453 thousand to 527 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is French Polynesia, at 4,167 km².

Cape Verde has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $2.6 billion in 2023, or $4,953 per person. That ranks 133rd of 213 by GDP per person.

Ivory Coast in numbers

Ivory Coast is the 71st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 50th largest by population.

It has roughly 101 people per square kilometre, about 1.7 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 84.8%, from 17.7 million to 32.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Norway, at 323,802 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Burkina Faso, Ghana, Guinea, Liberia and Mali.

The West African CFA franc is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $78.8 billion in 2023, or $2,528 per person. That ranks 161st of 213 by GDP per person.

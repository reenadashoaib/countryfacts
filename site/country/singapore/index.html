Singapore in numbers

Singapore is the 195th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 113th largest by population.

It has roughly 8,607 people per square kilometre, about 142.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 51.7%, from 4 million to 6.1 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Micronesia, at 702 km².

Singapore has no land borders with other countries.

The Singapore dollar is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $501.4 billion in 2023, or $84,734 per person. That ranks 11th of 213 by GDP per person.

Kosovo in numbers

Kosovo is the 167th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 153rd largest by population.

It has roughly 145 people per square kilometre, about 2.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 12.5%, from 1.8 million to 1.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Jamaica, at 10,991 km².

Kosovo is landlocked and shares land borders with 4 countries: Albania, North Macedonia, Montenegro and Serbia.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $10.4 billion in 2023, or $6,203 per person. That ranks 124th of 213 by GDP per person.

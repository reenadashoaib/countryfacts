Ecuador in numbers

Ecuador is the 75th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 70th largest by population.

It has roughly 66 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 44.1%, from 12.7 million to 18.3 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Burkina Faso, at 272,967 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Colombia and Peru.

The United States dollar is also used in 19 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $118.8 billion in 2023, or $6,610 per person. That ranks 120th of 213 by GDP per person.

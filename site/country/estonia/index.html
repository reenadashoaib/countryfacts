Estonia in numbers

Estonia is the 133rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 157th largest by population.

It has roughly 30 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 2.2%, from 1.4 million to 1.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Denmark, at 43,094 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Latvia and Russia.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $40.7 billion in 2023, or $29,735 per person. That ranks 52nd of 213 by GDP per person.

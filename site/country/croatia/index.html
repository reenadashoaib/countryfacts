Croatia in numbers

Croatia is the 128th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 130th largest by population.

It has roughly 68 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 13.3%, from 4.5 million to 3.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Togo, at 56,785 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Bosnia and Herzegovina, Hungary, Montenegro, Serbia and Slovenia.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $82.7 billion in 2023, or $21,424 per person. That ranks 68th of 213 by GDP per person.

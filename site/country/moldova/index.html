Moldova in numbers

Moldova is the 140th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 145th largest by population.

It has roughly 70 people per square kilometre, about 1.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 19.3%, from 2.9 million to 2.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Guinea-Bissau, at 36,125 km².

Moldova is landlocked and shares land borders with 2 countries: Romania and Ukraine.

Its economic output was about $16.5 billion in 2023, or $6,729 per person. That ranks 119th of 213 by GDP per person.

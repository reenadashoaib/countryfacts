Bangladesh in numbers

Bangladesh is the 95th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 8th largest by population.

It has roughly 1,191 people per square kilometre, about 19.7 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 30.6%, from 134.5 million to 175.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Nepal, at 147,181 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Myanmar and India.

Its economic output was about $437.4 billion in 2023, or $2,551 per person. That ranks 159th of 213 by GDP per person.

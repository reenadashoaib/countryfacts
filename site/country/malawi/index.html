Malawi in numbers

Malawi is the 101st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 60th largest by population.

It has roughly 188 people per square kilometre, about 3.1 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 96.3%, from 11.3 million to 22.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Eritrea, at 117,600 km².

Malawi is landlocked and shares land borders with 3 countries: Mozambique, Tanzania and Zambia.

Its economic output was about $14.1 billion in 2023, or $667 per person. That ranks 202nd of 213 by GDP per person.

Nicaragua in numbers

Nicaragua is the 99th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 108th largest by population.

It has roughly 54 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 39.5%, from 5 million to 7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Greece, at 131,990 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Costa Rica and Honduras.

Its economic output was about $17.8 billion in 2023, or $2,613 per person. That ranks 157th of 213 by GDP per person.

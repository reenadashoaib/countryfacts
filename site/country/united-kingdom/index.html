United Kingdom in numbers

United Kingdom is the 81st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 22nd largest by population.

It has roughly 286 people per square kilometre, about 4.7 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 18%, from 58.9 million to 69.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Uganda, at 241,550 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Ireland.

The British pound is also used in 5 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $3.34 trillion in 2023, or $48,741 per person. That ranks 30th of 213 by GDP per person.

Bermuda in numbers

Bermuda is the 234th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 199th largest by population.

It has roughly 1,195 people per square kilometre, about 19.8 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 4.9%, from 62 thousand to 65 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Saint Martin, at 53 km².

Bermuda has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $7.8 billion in 2022, or $120,897 per person. That ranks 4th of 213 by GDP per person.

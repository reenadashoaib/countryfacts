Tajikistan in numbers

Tajikistan is the 97th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 90th largest by population.

It has roughly 75 people per square kilometre, about 1.2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 71.6%, from 6.3 million to 10.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Nepal, at 147,181 km².

Tajikistan is landlocked and shares land borders with 4 countries: Afghanistan, China, Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan.

Its economic output was about $12.1 billion in 2023, or $1,161 per person. That ranks 189th of 213 by GDP per person.

Paraguay in numbers

Paraguay is the 61st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 107th largest by population.

It has roughly 17 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 37.5%, from 5.1 million to 7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Zimbabwe, at 390,757 km².

Paraguay is landlocked and shares land borders with 3 countries: Argentina, Bolivia and Brazil.

Its economic output was about $43 billion in 2023, or $6,276 per person. That ranks 123rd of 213 by GDP per person.

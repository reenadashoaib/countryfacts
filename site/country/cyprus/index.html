Cyprus in numbers

Cyprus is the 170th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 155th largest by population.

It has roughly 148 people per square kilometre, about 2.5 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 44.6%, from 948 thousand to 1.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Puerto Rico, at 8,870 km².

Cyprus has no land borders with other countries.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $32.2 billion in 2023, or $23,963 per person. That ranks 57th of 213 by GDP per person.

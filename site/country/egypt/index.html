Egypt in numbers

Egypt is the 31st largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 13th largest by population.

It has roughly 118 people per square kilometre, about 2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 62%, from 73.1 million to 118.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Mauritania, at 1,030,700 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Israel, Libya, Palestine and Sudan.

The Egyptian pound is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $395.9 billion in 2023, or $3,457 per person. That ranks 153rd of 213 by GDP per person.

Cameroon in numbers

Cameroon is the 55th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 51st largest by population.

It has roughly 63 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 100.4%, from 14.9 million to 29.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Turkmenistan, at 488,100 km².

It shares land borders with 6 countries: Central African Republic, Chad, Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Nigeria.

The Central African CFA franc is also used in 5 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $47.9 billion in 2023, or $1,690 per person. That ranks 176th of 213 by GDP per person.

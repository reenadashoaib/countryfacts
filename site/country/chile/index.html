Chile in numbers

Chile is the 39th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 65th largest by population.

It has roughly 26 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 28.1%, from 15.5 million to 19.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Zambia, at 752,612 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Argentina, Bolivia and Peru.

Its economic output was about $335.5 billion in 2023, or $17,068 per person. That ranks 78th of 213 by GDP per person.

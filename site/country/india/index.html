India in numbers

India is the 8th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 1st largest by population.

It has roughly 445 people per square kilometre, about 7.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 38.4%, from 1.1 billion to 1.5 billion in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Argentina, at 2,780,400 km².

It shares land borders with 6 countries: Bangladesh, Bhutan, Myanmar, China, Nepal and Pakistan.

The Indian rupee is also used in 2 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $3.55 trillion in 2023, or $2,469 per person. That ranks 163rd of 213 by GDP per person.

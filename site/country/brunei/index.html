Brunei in numbers

Brunei is the 174th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 173rd largest by population.

It has roughly 81 people per square kilometre, about 1.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 42.9%, from 326 thousand to 466 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Palestine, at 6,220 km².

It shares land borders with 1 country: Malaysia.

The Singapore dollar is also used in 1 other country or territory listed here.

Its economic output was about $15.1 billion in 2023, or $32,963 per person. That ranks 46th of 213 by GDP per person.

Greece in numbers

Greece is the 98th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 93rd largest by population.

It has roughly 79 people per square kilometre, about 1.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 3.6%, from 10.8 million to 10.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Nicaragua, at 130,373 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Albania, Bulgaria, Türkiye and North Macedonia.

The Euro is also used in 37 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $238.2 billion in 2023, or $22,888 per person. That ranks 60th of 213 by GDP per person.

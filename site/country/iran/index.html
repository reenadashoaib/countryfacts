Iran in numbers

Iran is the 19th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 17th largest by population.

It has roughly 56 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 39.1%, from 66.4 million to 92.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Mongolia, at 1,564,110 km².

It shares land borders with 7 countries: Afghanistan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Iraq, Pakistan, Türkiye and Turkmenistan.

Its economic output was about $401.5 billion in 2023, or $4,431 per person. That ranks 138th of 213 by GDP per person.

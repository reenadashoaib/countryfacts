Syria in numbers

Syria is the 90th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 57th largest by population.

It has roughly 138 people per square kilometre, about 2.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 54%, from 16.6 million to 25.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Cambodia, at 181,035 km².

It shares land borders with 5 countries: Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Lebanon and Türkiye.

Its economic output was about $9 billion in 2021, or $415 per person. That ranks 211th of 213 by GDP per person.

Poland in numbers

Poland is the 72nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 44th largest by population.

It has roughly 117 people per square kilometre, about 1.9 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 4.8%, from 38.3 million to 36.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Oman, at 309,500 km².

It shares land borders with 7 countries: Belarus, Czechia, Germany, Lithuania, Russia, Slovakia and Ukraine.

Its economic output was about $811.2 billion in 2023, or $22,112 per person. That ranks 64th of 213 by GDP per person.

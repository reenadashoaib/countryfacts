Cuba in numbers

Cuba is the 107th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 87th largest by population.

It has roughly 100 people per square kilometre, about 1.6 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 1.5%, from 11.1 million to 10.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Bulgaria, at 110,879 km².

Cuba has no land borders with other countries.

Its economic output was about $107.4 billion in 2020, or $9,605 per person. That ranks 101st of 213 by GDP per person.

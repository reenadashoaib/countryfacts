Angola in numbers

Angola is the 24th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 39th largest by population.

It has roughly 31 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 141.1%, from 16.2 million to 39 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Mali, at 1,240,192 km².

It shares land borders with 4 countries: Congo, DR Congo, Zambia and Namibia.

Its economic output was about $84.7 billion in 2023, or $2,305 per person. That ranks 164th of 213 by GDP per person.

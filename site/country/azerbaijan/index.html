Azerbaijan in numbers

Azerbaijan is the 115th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 94th largest by population.

It has roughly 118 people per square kilometre, about 2 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 27.3%, from 8 million to 10.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Serbia, at 88,361 km².

Azerbaijan is landlocked and shares land borders with 5 countries: Armenia, Georgia, Iran, Russia and Türkiye.

Its economic output was about $72.4 billion in 2023, or $7,126 per person. That ranks 115th of 213 by GDP per person.

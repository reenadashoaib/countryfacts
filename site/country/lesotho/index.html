Lesotho in numbers

Lesotho is the 142nd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 144th largest by population.

It has roughly 78 people per square kilometre, about 1.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 17.9%, from 2 million to 2.4 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Belgium, at 30,528 km².

Lesotho is landlocked and shares land borders with 1 country: South Africa.

The South African rand is also used in 4 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $2 billion in 2023, or $885 per person. That ranks 195th of 213 by GDP per person.

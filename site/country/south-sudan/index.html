South Sudan in numbers

South Sudan is the 45th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 80th largest by population.

It has roughly 20 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 102.1%, from 6 million to 12.2 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Central African Republic, at 622,984 km².

South Sudan is landlocked and shares land borders with 6 countries: Central African Republic, DR Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Sudan and Uganda.

Its economic output was about $12 billion in 2015, or $1,080 per person. That ranks 191st of 213 by GDP per person.

Venezuela in numbers

Venezuela is the 34th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 53rd largest by population.

It has roughly 31 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 16.3%, from 24.5 million to 28.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Nigeria, at 923,768 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Brazil, Colombia and Guyana.

Its economic output was about $482.4 billion in 2014, or $15,944 per person. That ranks 80th of 213 by GDP per person.

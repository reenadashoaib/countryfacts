Isle of Man in numbers

Isle of Man is the 198th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 195th largest by population.

It has roughly 147 people per square kilometre, about 2.4 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 11.2%, from 76 thousand to 84 thousand in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Guam, at 549 km².

Isle of Man has no land borders with other countries.

The British pound is also used in 5 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $7.9 billion in 2021, or $94,300 per person. That ranks 7th of 213 by GDP per person.

Hungary in numbers

Hungary is the 111th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 96th largest by population.

It has roughly 102 people per square kilometre, about 1.7 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has fallen by 6.8%, from 10.2 million to 9.5 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Portugal, at 92,090 km².

Hungary is landlocked and shares land borders with 7 countries: Austria, Croatia, Romania, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia and Ukraine.

Its economic output was about $212.4 billion in 2023, or $22,142 per person. That ranks 63rd of 213 by GDP per person.

North Korea in numbers

North Korea is the 100th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 56th largest by population.

It has roughly 220 people per square kilometre, about 3.7 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 12.3%, from 23.7 million to 26.6 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Malawi, at 118,484 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: China, South Korea and Russia.

Lebanon in numbers

Lebanon is the 169th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 115th largest by population.

It has roughly 560 people per square kilometre, about 9.3 times the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 35.1%, from 4.3 million to 5.8 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Gambia, at 10,689 km².

It shares land borders with 2 countries: Israel and Syria.

Its economic output was about $21 billion in 2022, or $3,654 per person. That ranks 150th of 213 by GDP per person.

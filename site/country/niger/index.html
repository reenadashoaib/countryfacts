Niger in numbers

Niger is the 23rd largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 54th largest by population.

It has roughly 22 people per square kilometre, well below the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 142.6%, from 11.5 million to 27.9 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Chad, at 1,284,000 km².

Niger is landlocked and shares land borders with 7 countries: Algeria, Benin, Burkina Faso, Chad, Libya, Mali and Nigeria.

The West African CFA franc is also used in 7 other countries and territories listed here.

Its economic output was about $16.8 billion in 2023, or $643 per person. That ranks 203rd of 213 by GDP per person.

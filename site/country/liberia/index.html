Liberia in numbers

Liberia is the 105th largest of the 249 countries and territories on this site by area, and the 116th largest by population.

It has roughly 51 people per square kilometre, close to the world average of 60.

Since 2000 its population has grown by 95.7%, from 2.9 million to 5.7 million in 2025.

The country closest to it in size is Bulgaria, at 110,879 km².

It shares land borders with 3 countries: Guinea, Ivory Coast and Sierra Leone.

Its economic output was about $4.3 billion in 2023, or $789 per person. That ranks 199th of 213 by GDP per person.
